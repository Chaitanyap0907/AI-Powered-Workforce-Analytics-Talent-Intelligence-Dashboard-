Leave of Absence Policy

Medical Leave: Employees requiring extended medical leave may take up to
12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave under FMLA, provided they have
worked at least 1,250 hours in the preceding 12 months.

Bereavement Leave: Employees are granted 5 paid days of bereavement leave
for the loss of an immediate family member, and 2 paid days for extended
family members.

Jury Duty: Employees called for jury duty receive full pay for up to
10 business days, with documentation from the court required.
