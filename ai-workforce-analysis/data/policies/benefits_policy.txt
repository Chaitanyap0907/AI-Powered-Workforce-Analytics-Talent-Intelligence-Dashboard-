Employee Benefits Policy

Health Insurance: All full-time employees are eligible for company-sponsored
health insurance starting on their first day of employment. The company covers
80% of the premium for employees and 50% for dependents. Enrollment must occur
within 30 days of hire date or during the annual open enrollment period.

Paid Time Off (PTO): Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per year during
their first 3 years of employment, increasing to 20 days after 3 years and
25 days after 7 years. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis.

Parental Leave: Employees are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave
following the birth, adoption, or fostering of a child, provided they have
been employed for at least 6 months prior to the leave start date.

Retirement Plan: The company offers a 401(k) plan with a 4% match, vested
immediately. Employees may enroll at any time after their 90-day probation
period ends.

Professional Development: Employees may request up to $2,000 per year in
tuition reimbursement or conference attendance, subject to manager approval
and a demonstrated connection to their current role.
